Special Laws

These laws control what happens to the AXR in certain situations and conditions.

Order Flow

  • 3.1.1 – The Lord, Admiral, and Allegiant General may order: Directors, Generals, Commanders, and Soldiers. In emergencies they may order: Presidents, Regional Sub-Leaders, and Civil[...]
  • 3.1.2 - Presidents may order: Soldiers, and Regional Sub-Leaders. In emergencies they may order: Civilians.
  • 3.1.3 - The Civil Director may order: Soldiers, Civilians (In Project).
  • 3.1.4 - The Militant Director may order: Soldiers, Civilians (In Project). In emergencies they may order: Presidents, Generals, Commanders, Regional Sub-Leaders, and Civilians.
  • 3.1.5 - Generals may order: Commanders, and Soldiers. In emergencies they may order: Presidents, Regional Sub-Leaders, and Civilians.
  • 3.1.6 - Commanders may order: Soldiers. In emergencies they may order: Regional Sub-Leaders, and Civilians.
  • 3.1.7 - In emergencies Regional Sub-Leaders may order: Civilians.
  • 3.1.8 - Orders can be modified by a superior of the orderer.
  • 3.1.9 - Orders from higher ranking-members (by level) are to be prioritised.
  • 3.1.10 - Regional Sub-Leaders must follow the same laws as Civilians.

Administration Tree

  • 3.2.1 Orders flow downward in hierarchical format (please check the main document for details)

Overruling

  • 3.3.1 - The Lord may be overruled if: The Admiral, Allegiant General, all Presidents, and Generals wish to make the Admiral the new Lord.
  • 3.3.2 - During an emergency: the Admiral, and Allegiant General are placed below the Lord, and the Lord may order them as he wishes.
  • 3.3.3 - If the Lord is unable to rule the succession will run in this order: Admiral > Allegiant General > Militant Director > President of Axia > 1st General > 2nd General > 3rd Gene[...]

Re-ranking

  • 3.4.1 - The Lord, Admiral, and Allegiant General may change the rank of any member of the AXR.
  • 3.4.2 - Presidents may appoint or remove Regional Sub-Leaders with permission from the Lord, Admiral, or Allegiant General.
  • 3.4.3 - Generals may appoint or remove Commanders with permission from the Lord, Admiral, or Allegiant General.

Reforming

  • 3.5.1 - If the AXR is dismantled by declaration from the Lord all AXR laws within this document will be discarded, all ranks will be removed and will lose authority.
  • 3.5.2 - During reformation the Lord will lose his title and merely be "Axo".
  • 3.5.3 - During reformation the Admiral will lose his title and merely be "23".
  • 3.5.4 - During reformation the Allegiant General will lose his title and merely be "51".

Policy

These are policies for the public to read and understand why we have these laws and what to do in return to make the AXR a better place.

Document Policies

  • You are expected to follow these laws, no matter prefecture or province.
  • You must appreciate these laws and the lengths taken to enforce them to the benefit of the public.

Web Policies

  • You have access to contact the AXR through official channels; we have zero tolerance for any abuse or harassment of our admins using those means.
  • There is copyright on most AXR media, please do not ignore this and follow the licensing we give you.
  • The AXR does not have presence on all sites, and please do not pretend to be an official AXR channel.

Terms and Conditions

  • By joining the AXR, you agree to be part of the chain of command.
  • You agree to take responsibility for what you do and take punishment.
