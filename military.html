AXR Military

We assume you are here because you are interested in joining (or just having a look). The highest rank in the military is the Militant Director. To achieve this rank you must have successfully led a legion and demonstrated exceptional military prowess.

The AXR military is divided into three legions, each with its own specialized divisions and command structure.

1st Legion

General: TacoyTacobell

The 1st Legion serves as the primary military force of the AXR, commanding ground, air, and sea operations.

2nd Legion

General: Kramby79

The 2nd Legion specializes in advanced tactical operations and strategic deployment across AXR territories.

3rd Legion

General: Vacant

The 3rd Legion position is currently open. Qualified commanders are encouraged to apply for leadership of this strategic force.

Joining the Military

If you're interested in joining the AXR military, contact us through our official channels listed on the home page. We're always looking for dedicated individuals to strengthen our forces.
