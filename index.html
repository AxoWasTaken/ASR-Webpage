Axos Republic

Official Website of the AXR

Welcome

Welcome to the official AXR website! The Axos Republic is a Minecraft empire based on the server REDACTED. Founded on June 15th 2025 by Lordos Axos (AxoFX), the capital of the AXR is Axford — one of the largest and most advanced cities on the server.

Ranking

The AXR has a complex ranking system based around legions and divisions. At the very top are the Lord, Admiral, and Alligant General, who lead the AXR to a brighter future.

Below them are the Militant Director and Civil Director, followed by the Generals, each commanding a legion.

Each legion contains three divisions:

  • Ground Force
  • Air Force
  • Sea Force

Within these divisions are Soldiers, trained for their environments. Alongside them are Civilians, the most vital part of the AXR.

Prefecture

The AXR consists of Prefectures — smaller districts of the greater empire. The main Prefecture is Axfordshire, surrounding Axford. Each Prefecture has its own sub‑capital but is administered directly from Axford.

Road classes are marked by coloured stripes:

  • Red — Small, 2 lanes
  • Orange — Large, 4 lanes
  • Yellow — Speedway, 2 ice tracks
  • Unclassed — Rare, usually 2 lanes, no stripe

Media

The AXR produces documents, spreadsheets, images, and videos. Many are protected by copyright. Classified documents will be marked with "CLASSIFIED" and a minimum rank requirement.
