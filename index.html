Official Website of the UCA

Welcome

Welcome to the official UCA website! The United Colonies of Arisk is a Minecraft alliance based on the server REDACTED. The capital of the UCA is Hillside, possibly the oldest and largest country on the server.

Governance

The UCA is led as a representative democracy by the Presidents (sometimes other titles) of the countries it consists of. Each President has a Secretary (yet again sometimes another title), who acts as an assistant for them. The UCA has one Militant Director who leads the soldiers from all countries. Each President also has a number of Regional Sub-Leaders, who lead the Oblasts (regions) of each country.

Media

The UCA has a group known as the Axian Media Coalition, who produce media such as books, videos, etc. Most of these are publicly available in libraries and shops across the UCA. Some of the content has copyright on it so please don't violate it. The UCA also has its own classified media, such as plans for military operations or black projects, these documents will be clearly marked with a mark saying 'CLASSIFIED LVL#', the higher the "LVL" (eg. LVL3, LVL4, LVL5) the more classified it is. If you come across any you are not supposed to have access to delete them from your computer and notify Lordos Axos (or any other high-ranking officer).
